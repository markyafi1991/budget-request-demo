# Project Brief

**Version 0.1.0** · 2026-11-12 · Scaffold / not a completed demo

## Decision and audience

Demonstrate how a department spending request affects a simple budget view. Fictional software-services company; portfolio setting only. This is the small Austin demo, separate from the larger SaaS Driver-Based Forecast Pack. Target presentation date is Monday 2026-11-16; completion is not asserted.

## Scope

Two Power Apps screens: Request and Review. One Power BI page: budget, already spent, approved requests, pending requests, available budget, department filter and status breakdown. One synthetic month, three departments and four starting requests. Manual review/status change; no automated approval flow in the first version.
